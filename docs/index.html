Draft Room

Advanced-analytics squad building for the EuroLeague Fantasy Challenge — projections, an exact optimiser, an MCTS draft lookahead, and a matchup-aware weekly transfer engine.

Scout & Analysis →Projections, scouting radars, club depth charts, and three backtests. Squad Room →Weekly transfer optimiser and the live draft board, with two engines.

Runs entirely in your browser — no install or server needed. Open the Squad Room and use your browser's Install app (the ⤓ button, or the address-bar install icon) to keep it on your dock and use it offline. Your teams and drafts are saved on this device.
